# Code of conduct

MedAgentRx brings together clinicians, quality and regulatory specialists, statisticians and engineers. We want everyone to be able to take part.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Explain clearly, without jargon where possible, for readers from other fields.
- Give and accept critique of work, not of people.
- Respect privacy. Never share patient data or confidential regulatory material.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable in any project space.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may warn, suspend or remove anyone who breaks this code.
